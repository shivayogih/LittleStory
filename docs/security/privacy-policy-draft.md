LittleStory is a storytelling app for children aged 3 to 12, set up and managed by a parent or guardian. It is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we").

## Children's information

We do not collect personal information from children or from parents. When a parent sets up a child profile, the child's first name, age, avatar, interests and preferred language are saved only in the app's private storage on that device, together with favourites, reading and listening progress, downloads and stories created in the app. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. This version of LittleStory does not connect to the internet.

## Advertising, tracking and purchases

LittleStory shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking tools, does not read the advertising ID or other device identifiers, and processes no payments. The subscription screen is an illustration only.

## Story creation

Stories made in the app are put together on the device from a built-in template. No artificial intelligence service is used, and what you type is not sent anywhere.

## Keeping and deleting information

Information stays on the device until you delete it or uninstall the app. It is excluded from cloud backups and from transfers to a new device. A parent can change a child's details in **Profile › Manage children** and delete everything in **Profile › Privacy & parent controls › Delete all data**. These areas ask a question a grown-up can answer before they open.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version connects to the internet, uses an AI service to write stories, or collects any information, we will update this policy before that version is released and ask for a parent's verifiable consent before any child's information leaves the device.
